Enuma has no author. It is written by the people who watch it on the channel. There are two ways to leave a mark.

1. Vote on a law

  1. A vote Short asks a question about the world and shows three answers: A, B and C.
  2. Comment the letter of your choice. Add a reason if you like -- a good reason can become canon too.
  3. One person is one ballot, however many times they comment. Likes on a comment add a little weight and break ties.
  4. A vote stays open at least a day, and until enough people have voted. Then the winner becomes a law, with its own entry and Short.

2. Propose a piece of the world

  1. Under any Short, describe something that should exist: a place, a people, a machine, a secret.
  2. The best fitting idea is checked against the laws and the existing canon, so the world never contradicts itself.
  3. If it fits it becomes an entry, with Proposed by @you in the Short, on this wiki and in a reply to your comment.
