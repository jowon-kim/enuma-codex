Silent Resonance Keepers

The Chrono-Scribes guard the Luminous Library, curating spoken myths to steer reality. They edit the Chrono-Mythic Resonance, preserving favored histories while erasing others.

Facts

  • Chrono-Scribes train members to recite legends with precise cadence.
  • The Luminous Library's Atrium amplifies Chrono-Mythic Resonance tenfold.
  • Erasing a myth removes its influence; if done without proper rites it can destabilize nearby timelines.
  • Only the Council of Echoes can authorize a mythic purge.
