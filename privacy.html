Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-29

Enuma is a YouTube channel and this wiki. An automated tool run by the channel owner publishes the channel's videos. This page explains what that tool does with data.

What the tool accesses

  • The channel owner's own YouTube account, through Google OAuth, to upload videos to the channel.
  • Public comments posted under the channel's own videos, to count votes and read proposed ideas (YouTube Data API commentThreads.list).
  • Replies it posts under those comments, to tell a viewer their idea became canon (comments.insert).
  • Public view counts of the channel's own videos (videos.list), to see which topics people watch.

It does not access any viewer's Google account, email address, private data or anything outside this channel.

What is stored and shown

When a viewer's comment is used in the world, its public display name and the comment text may be stored in the canon and shown in videos, on this wiki and in video descriptions as credit. Nothing else about viewers is stored. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Removal

To have your name or comment removed from the canon and credits, contact zxvj2004@gmail.com. You can also delete your comment on YouTube at any time.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The channel also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.
